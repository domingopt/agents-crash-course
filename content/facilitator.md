# Facilitator notes

These notes are for you, the person running the session, not for the learner. They're linked quietly from the site footer and aren't part of the course route.

## Before the session

- **Personalise.** In `content/site.json`, check `learnerName`, and fill in `businessDefaults` (agency name, host agency, network marketing company, sign-off) if you know them. Anything left blank she can fill in herself in the Agent Builder.
- **Re-check prices.** Open module 07 and spot-check each platform's price against its own pricing page. Update `content/platforms.json` and `lastChecked` in both `platforms.json` and `site.json`.
- **Fresh start.** On the device you'll present from, use "Reset progress for a fresh run-through" at the top of this page.
- **Her devices.** Open the site on her phone at the start so it's in her browser history. Her saved agents live on whichever device she builds them on, so suggest she builds on the laptop she'll use for the business.
- **Print** two copies of the cheat sheet (footer → Cheat sheet → Print).
- **Bring** a non-work laptop. Never demo on an NHS device.

## The 90-minute walkthrough

You're not setting anything up with her today. The goal is that she understands the ideas, has made a platform decision she's comfortable with (or knows how to make one), and has 2–3 agents saved in "My agents", ready to launch when she chooses.

### 0–5 min · Welcome

- Explain the shape: learn, decide, build. Everything she types stays on her device: no account, no tracking.
- Show the progress stubs along the top and the glossary button.

### 5–15 min · Module 01: Your week, reclaimed

- Let her answer the warm-up herself. Be honest that the hours are illustrative.
- Toggle "With agents" and let the animation land.
- **Pause:** "Which of these jobs do you dread most on a Tuesday night?" Note the answer; it's probably her first agent.

### 15–30 min · Module 02: What is an agent?

- Click through chatbot → automation → agent. The analogies (friend / standing order / assistant) do the heavy lifting.
- Step through the agent loop slowly, using Next rather than autoplay, so you can talk over it.
- **Pause:** ask her to explain an agent back to you in her own words, and how it differs from ChatGPT as she uses it today. (Learning outcome 1.)

### 30–40 min · Module 03: The building blocks

- Let her tap each of the six parts. Spend the most time on Instructions and Guardrails.
- **Pause:** "If one of these went wrong, which would worry you most?" Usually guardrails, which leads nicely into safety later.

### 40–50 min · Module 04: Good at, bad at

- She drives the sorter. Disagreements are the best part, so discuss them rather than rushing on.
- Make sure hallucination and out-of-date prices land. Use the visa example.

### 50–55 min · Module 05: Ideas gallery

- Quick skim. Ask her to pick 2–3 cards that would help most; jot them on the cheat sheet.

### 55–65 min · Module 06: Safety and the rules

- **Slow down on NHS separation.** This is the one non-negotiable.
- Do the traffic-light quiz together.
- **Pause:** check she's comfortable with the "agents draft, you send" rule for network marketing messages.

### 65–80 min · Module 07: Choosing your platform

- She answers the five filter questions. If she doesn't know which email she uses, open the "Not sure?" box together.
- Read the trade-offs out loud. Resist recommending a winner; ask "which downside could you live with?"
- Look at the cost-benefit table with her own numbers.
- It's fine to leave without deciding. The builder lets her generate packs for any platform and switch later.

### 80–88 min · Module 08: Agent Builder

- Build one template agent with her end to end, through to the pack. "Business in 15 minutes" is the quickest.
- Save it to My agents. Show Edit, Duplicate, Export and Print.
- If time allows, start a second from one of her gallery picks and let her finish it at home.

### 88–90 min · Module 09: Your first 30 days

- Walk through week 1 only. Hand over the printed cheat sheet.
- Remind her: export a backup of My agents after building.

## Questions she might ask

- **"Is it safe to connect my email?"** Yes, if it's a business-only inbox, the connection is 'read and draft', and she reviews drafts. She can disconnect any time.
- **"Will it send things without me knowing?"** Not if she keeps the "Always ask me before anything is sent" rule and only grants draft permissions. Every pack sets it up that way.
- **"What if the AI gets a price wrong?"** It will, sometimes. That's why prices are always "to be confirmed with the supplier".
- **"Can it do my WhatsApp?"** Not personal WhatsApp. It drafts; she pastes. See the note in module 07.
- **"Which one would you choose?"** Share your view if asked, but make the trade-offs explicit and let the filter do the work.

## After the session

- Send her the link (it's unlisted, so it won't appear in search engines).
- Suggest she does the week 1 tasks within 7 days, while it's fresh.
- If you update content later (prices, new platforms), she'll see the changes next time she visits; her saved agents aren't affected.
